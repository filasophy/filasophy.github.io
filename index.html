How We Work

Rules from the stage

Accept the premise.

AI is already in your building. Let's start there, with what's actually in the room, and build from it.

Yes, and.

Every school already has a game going: a teacher's clever workaround, a team's shortcut. Let's find it and build on it. Often the answer already lives in your tech stack, and the real work is the workflow around it. When a new tool joins the scene, it's because it makes your people look good. That's how one school said yes to Playlab: within ten weeks, a teacher-built app was running in four classes.

Play first.

Services

What We Do Together

01

AI Tool Vetting & Governance

For schools adding AI tools faster than anyone can track them

Most schools can't say which apps students use, who approved them, or what data those apps collect. I fix that. Together we audit what you already run, build approval criteria your staff will actually use, and set up a review cycle that keeps working after I leave. You get a living inventory, a clear approval process, and the people trained to run it. Read the case study

02

Adoption Measurement

For leaders who need evidence for their board, their budget, or themselves

Usage numbers tell you people logged in; they don't tell you whether anything changed. I design a baseline study before your AI rollout, then measure again after training and support, so you see what moved and what didn't. You get a findings report, a professional learning plan built from the results, and a second measurement six to nine months later. Your data stays yours.
